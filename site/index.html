Hold the bonus.
Prove the price.

Liquidating a loan pays the liquidator a bonus, on whatever price the feed published. Lantern holds that bonus back for five minutes, so anyone can prove from on-chain records that the price contradicted the feed's own history or its declared second source, and be paid out of the feed's own bond for proving it.
