How it works

The debt settles. The profit waits.

Checking a price before the liquidation means knowing the true price. Afterwards is easier: the feed has published a history and its signer has posted collateral behind it.

Step 01 · Liquidate

The market liquidates as it always did.

Repayment and the closing of the position happen at the moment of liquidation. The market hands Lantern only the liquidator's bonus, plus the exposure the liquidation put at risk.

Step 02 · Hold

The bonus sits behind a window.

The window and the bounty share are constructor arguments. No one can shorten the window, lengthen it, or reach into the escrow while it runs.

Step 03 · Prove

Anyone may accuse, with a stake.

A challenger names a rule and supplies evidence, not an answer. At adjudication the contract recomputes the verdict from state it already holds, so a wrong accusation costs the stake.

Step 04 · Settle

The money goes where the proof says.

Proof holds: the bonus returns to the borrower, the signer's bond pays the prover, and the feed must carry 20% more before it prints again. No proof: the liquidator is paid in full.

What a challenger can prove

Five rules. Each one a predicate over state.

No reference price set by hand. No committee. The challenger supplies the rule and the evidence; the contract decides.

Two values, one round SLOT_UNIQUENESS Stale when consumed ROUND_ORDERING Outside its own band SELF_HISTORY
Payload signed for another slot PAYLOAD_PROVENANCE A declared peer disagrees CROSS_SOURCE
Printing is free Pricing needs ≥ 4 prints Bond ≥ exposure +20% per caught print
RULE 0

SLOT_UNIQUENESS

One feed printed two different values for one round. The conflict is recorded rather than hidden behind a revert.

RULE 1

ROUND_ORDERING

The print was already stale, or out of order, when the liquidation consumed it.

RULE 2

SELF_HISTORY

The value falls outside the band implied by the feed's own realized moves, and nothing else.

RULE 3

PAYLOAD_PROVENANCE

The signed payload was issued for a different asset or a different slot.

RULE 4

CROSS_SOURCE

A second feed, declared in advance and fixed forever, disagrees beyond 5% on the same round.

The bond answers for the position

Recovery isn't capped by the profit.

A bond sized against the bonus can only give back the bonus. The market reports what the liquidation put at risk, and the bond must cover it.

  • Bond ≥ exposure, 1:1, and ≥ 1% of the notional.
  • Each caught print raises the requirement 20%, up to 3×.
  • A feed that can't meet its requirement can't price a liquidation.

Printing is free, pricing is not

A band from one print excuses anything.

An attacker would arrange exactly that before printing a number they invented. So a liquidation may only be priced on a print with at least four behind it, snapshotted on the report itself.

  • A feed below the floor keeps printing freely; that is how it builds history.
  • The count is stored per report, never recomputed later.
  • Floors scale to the asset's decimals; fee-on-transfer tokens are refused.
