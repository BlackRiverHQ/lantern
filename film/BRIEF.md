# BRIEF — Lantern launch film

- **What it's for:** Lantern, a Solidity protocol live on Arbitrum Sepolia. A liquidator's bonus is held
  for five minutes; anyone can prove the price behind the liquidation was false and send the bonus to
  the borrower, paid for out of the feed's bond.
- **Who's watching:** DeFi protocol engineers, lending-market and oracle people, hackathon judges. They
  know what a liquidation and an oracle are. They care whether a bad print can be undone without a
  committee, an admin key, or a hand-set reference price, and whether it is actually deployed.
- **What they should do at the end:** open the live dashboard and run a case themselves
  (friendly-fennec-31.convex.site/dashboard).
- **Length / sizes:** 30 s. 16:9 (1920×1080) master, 9:16 (1080×1920) re-laid-out from the same timeline.
  60 fps.
- **Brand (from site/):** background #191919 (black-03), card #212121/#282828, light surface #f8f8f8,
  lime #ddff46 (primary accent), pink #ff35a2 (the false print), blue #405bff (bond), green #a9ff5e (sparingly).
  Display: headingFont3 (site h1, bold geometric, tracking −0.05em). Body: bodyFont. Data: monoFont.
  Logo: the "Lantern" wordmark set in headingFont3 700 (the site's own SVG is text in that face).
- **Facts file:** `FACTS.md`. Only source for on-screen numbers, names and claims.
- **Assets:** real screenshots of the live dashboard (assets/shots/*.png, captured 2026-10-03 at 2× DPR).
  No footage, no generated imagery.
- **Style references:** none supplied. Using the kit's motion grammar and launch-film notes
  (Contra 24 foreground fly-through, Work Louder 22 exploded layers, Extend 28 registered decomposition,
  Bolt 25 strike/replace type, Replit 11 materialising result).
- **Music:** Mixkit "Tech House vibes" (#130), 123.05 BPM, Mixkit free licence. Beat = 0.4876 s, bar = 1.9505 s.
  Sparser intro for 8 bars, groove arrives at bar 8 = film 15.60 s, which is where the verdict lands.
- **Never claim:** mainnet, users, TVL, audits, partners, "secure", "guaranteed", any loan size for case #45.
